# Shifa RAG — Evaluation Report

_Generated by `evaluation.run_evaluation`. This is a research benchmark for retrieval and generation experimentation on educational medical summaries — it is **not** a clinical validation study, and results must not be presented as clinical evidence._

## 1. Benchmark overview

- **Corpus**: 80 original educational medical documents (~19,453 words, 20 topic areas).
- **Questions**: 195 QA pairs (150 single-document, 45 multi-document), each annotated with an expected answer and the relevant document(s).
- **Relevance level**: document-level. A retrieved chunk is a hit if its originating document is in the question's relevant set.
- **Metrics**: Recall@5, Recall@10, Precision@5, Precision@10, MRR@10, nDCG@10 (retrieval); faithfulness, hallucination rate, answer relevance, context relevance (generation).

Run configuration: `embedding_models=['lsa-25', 'lsa-50', 'ngram-hash-512']`, `chunk_sizes=[256, 512, 768, 1024]`, `retrieval_methods=['bm25', 'dense', 'hybrid']`, `max_queries=all`

## 6. Findings & how to read them

- Retrieval metrics answer **which retriever/config finds the right documents**; generation metrics answer **whether the answer is grounded in what was retrieved**.
- A high Recall@10 with low Recall@5 means the right document is in the pool but not ranked near the top — a reranking or fusion opportunity.
- Hallucination rate is the fraction of answer claims unsupported by the retrieved context. Compare it across generators with retrieval held fixed.
- This benchmark is a **controlled research harness**, not a clinical evaluation. It should be described as domain-specific QA/retrieval experimentation, not medical diagnosis validation.
